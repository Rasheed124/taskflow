Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskFlow collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our application.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

TaskFlow is a task management application designed to help individuals organize tasks, manage categories and keep track of their work.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy describes the information we collect and how it is used when you access TaskFlow.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect information such as:

  • Username.
  • Email address.
  • Password credentials, stored using Django's password-hashing mechanisms rather than as plain text.
  • Email verification and account status information.

Task and application information

When you use TaskFlow, we process information you provide, including:

  • Task titles and descriptions.
  • Task categories.
  • Task status, priority and relevant dates.
  • Other information you enter while managing your tasks.

Technical information

Our hosting and application infrastructure may process technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. This may include request information, timestamps and error logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Create and maintain your TaskFlow account.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Store, display and manage your tasks and categories.
  • Send registration verification emails.
  • Process password reset and account-related email requests.
  • Verify email address changes.
  • Maintain application security and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Improve the reliability and operation of TaskFlow.

4. Gmail API and Transactional Emails

TaskFlow uses Google's Gmail API to send transactional emails, including account verification, password recovery and other account-related notifications.

For this purpose, TaskFlow connects to an authorized Gmail sending account using Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization system. The Gmail permission requested is:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send

This permission allows TaskFlow to send email through the authorized sending account. TaskFlow does not use this permission to read, search, download or manage your personal Gmail inbox or messages.

Email addresses and message content necessary to deliver transactional messages are processed for that purpose. Google processes the relevant information when providing its Gmail API service.

TaskFlow does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to the email functionality described in this policy.

TaskFlow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may process or share information with service providers when necessary to operate TaskFlow, including:

  • Render: Application hosting and infrastructure used to operate the TaskFlow service.
  • Google: Gmail API services used to send transactional emails.

These providers process information as necessary to provide their respective services and under their applicable terms and privacy policies.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users or our legal rights.

6. Data Storage and Security

TaskFlow uses application and database infrastructure to store account and task information. Production data is hosted using the application's configured hosting and database services.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

These measures include password hashing, authentication, user-level access controls, secure application configuration and HTTPS in production.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their login credentials confidential.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and task information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account functionality, address security issues and meet applicable legal obligations.

Information may remain in backups or operational records for a limited period after changes or deletion, where applicable.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can access and update certain account information through your TaskFlow account.

You may contact us to ask questions about your personal information or request access, correction or deletion where applicable law provides those rights.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and legitimate service needs.

9. Cookies and Sessions

TaskFlow uses cookies and session-related technologies required for authentication, session management and security. These include Django session and CSRF protection mechanisms.

These technologies help maintain your authenticated session and protect forms and account operations.

10. Children's Privacy

TaskFlow is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as TaskFlow's features, infrastructure or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date.
